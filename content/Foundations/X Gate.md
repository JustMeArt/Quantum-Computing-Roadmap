# X Gate (bit-flip)
#qc/gates

Flips a qubit: |0⟩→|1⟩, |1⟩→|0⟩. Quantum analog of classical NOT. Geometrically: 180° rotation around the X-axis of the [[Bloch Sphere]].

$$X = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$$
